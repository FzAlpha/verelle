Manifesto

Good design is as little design as possible. We reduce until only the essential remains — type, grid, image and the space between them. Clarity is not a style. It is a form of respect for the reader.

Josef Müller-Brockmann taught that the grid makes the work objective. Twenty-eight years later, it is still the starting point of every project we take on.

Principles

Four rules.
No exceptions.

01

Grid

Every element earns its position. A modular structure turns intuition into a system others can continue.

02

Type

One family, few sizes, honest weights. Hierarchy comes from scale and space, never from ornament.

03

Reduction

We remove until nothing more can go. What remains carries the message with full force.

04

Objectivity

Photography over illustration, fact over decoration. The content speaks; we make it legible.
